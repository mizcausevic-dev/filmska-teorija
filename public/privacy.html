Privacy

Filmska Teorija stores media URLs, scene tags, reader links, and essay notes in the browser's localStorage on the device using the app. This static build does not include accounts, a server database, a payment flow, or hidden community submission.

Google Analytics 4 and AdSense are not active unless a future deployment adds real IDs and consent-aware configuration. Embedded third-party media may be governed by the third party's privacy policy when loaded.
