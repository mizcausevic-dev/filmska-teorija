# Filmska Teorija

Filmska Teorija is an interactive source-backed film theory learning app.

Primary URL: https://mizcausevic-dev.github.io/filmska-teorija/
Repository: https://github.com/mizcausevic-dev/filmska-teorija

Source posture:
- The core module registry is generated from the Wikipedia API for the user-supplied film theory pages.
- Wikipedia text is attributed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike License 4.0 unless otherwise noted.
- The app labels redirects, including Cognitive film theory resolving to Cognitivism (aesthetics), and Screen theory resolving to Marxist film theory.
- Local media URLs, scene breakdowns, reader links, and essay notes are browser-local in this static build.
- Community boards and AI partner behavior are local prototypes, not a live moderated network or live model service.

Important files:
- /src/data/wikipediaSources.json
- /scripts/fetch-wikipedia-sources.mjs
